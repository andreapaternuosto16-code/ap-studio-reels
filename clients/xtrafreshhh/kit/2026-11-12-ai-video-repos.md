## How we use them (starting with XtraFreshhh social)
1. HyperFrames + Herk kit in a coding chat ("AP/BUILD — XtraFreshhh — reel templates"): 3–4 vertical 1080×1920 templates, 8–15 s, in XF's brand (mint/teal, her logo PNG as-is): before→after wipe, "price in 10 seconds", 3 tips, review quote card. Text and photos are slots, so each week's post is a fill-in, not a new build.
2. Images: AP/IMAGE STUDIO (ChatGPT) makes the stills/backgrounds from briefs (bright daytime, evenly lit); HyperFrames animates them (parallax, wipe, counters). Her real photos first where we have them.
3. Music: royalty-free only (OpenFilm library / HyperFrames sounds), noted per post.
4. Wording: every caption through the wording reviewer, as now.
5. Later: P3 (project reveals), AYA (tax tips explainers), AP Studio's own reels and the dashboard showcase film.
