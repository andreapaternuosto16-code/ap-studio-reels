# Using the reel assets

**A — “POV: you booked the clean”:** six portrait stills and 07-pan-strip. **B — Christmas countdown:** five portrait stills and 06-pan-strip. **C — message/review overlays:** five portrait stills. Phone screens are blank. B01 and C01 have calm wall space for overlays. Filenames appear only on contact sheets, not in production images.

Use plain .png files for standard resolution and -hd.png files for HD. Portraits are 1080×1920 / 2160×3840. Pan strips are 4320×1920 / 8640×3840; pan across the single continuous image. HD photographs are enlarged from the recorded smaller natives.

**D/cutouts:** nine transparent sets on 2000×2000 canvases. **D/shadows:** six matching, separate soft shadow layers. Place each shadow beneath its matching cutout at the same position and scale. They are silhouette projections, so opacity and position can be adjusted independently. Glints, bubbles and spray mist do not need ground shadows.

**D/effect-parts:** five separate glints and six separate bubbles on transparent 2000×2000 canvases. The bubbles retain different visible diameters under a common scale. The grouped sets remain available too.

**D/mirror-wipe/frame-01…06:** ordered 1080×1920 frames, with double-size HD versions. They illustrate a generic wipe, not a customer's job or proof of cleaning performance. The cloth moves from left to right; folds vary between keyframes. Interpolate or crossfade as needed. WIPE-PREVIEW.gif is a 270×480 reference only. The bathroom outside the mirror glass is locked to the same base pixels. sources/MIRROR-ASSEMBLY.json records the aperture and approximate clearing stages.

Exact prompts, references, natives and QA are in sources/ and qa/. Python recipes run from the AP STUDIO workspace with Pillow installed. Nothing has been published or scheduled.
