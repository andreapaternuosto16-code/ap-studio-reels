# XtraFreshhh reels — what works + engaging styles we can build
_Last written: 2026-11-12 17:05 by Claude (AP/XTRAFRESHHH)_ — Andrea 16:47: round-1 reels (PR #1, ap-studio-reels) are "okay but boring"; the single-review reel is too thin.

## What the research says (cleaning + Reels, 2026)
- Hook in the first 2–3 s; Reels of 15–30 s get the most engagement; 1080×1920, sharp. (Socialinsider, 160K accounts)
- Best formats: how-to/tips, behind the scenes, trending audio; humour gets 2–3× shares; real customer content lifts engagement up to ~50%.
- Cleaning specifically: real transformations (start on the "before"), satisfying sped-up cleans, meet-the-team, tool-of-the-week, FAQ answers with the question on screen, testimonial montages; captions always (sound-off viewing).
- Shift: over-polished, staged, product-stacked "CleanTok" is losing views (−50–90%); practical fixes, calm sensory moments, realistic routines and natural products are what hold attention now.
- Takeaway for us: motion graphics alone over still photos will always feel a bit flat. The strongest reels mix (a) her REAL footage/photos (team, a wipe, a finished room, a happy message) with (b) our animated layer (captions, reveals, counters, transitions). Ask Alexandra for 5–10 s phone clips.

## Why round 1 felt boring
One slow photo + text appearing = no change on screen for 2–4 s at a time. Engaging reels change something every 1–2 s (cut, camera move, reveal), open on movement + a question, and end with a reason to comment/share.

## Six reel concepts (each a different look, no two weeks the same)
1. **"POV: you booked a clean"** — phone buzz → booking-request notification → whip-pans through 4 rooms, each "ticked" as it lands, beat-cut to music → calm finished living room → CTA. ~15 s.
2. **"Can you spot what guests notice first?"** — a room photo, a 3-2-1 countdown, then circles pop on 5 details (mat, towels, hob, mirror, bins) with one-word labels; ask "How many did you get?" (comments). ~15 s.
3. **"Messages we love"** (replaces the single-review reel) — real Google reviews (with permission) arriving as a cascade of phone notifications, fast, 5–6 of them, then a count-up of real total reviews (checked live), then "Here's what they mean" → 3 quick room shots → CTA. ~20 s.
4. **Christmas countdown board** — split-flap board flips "DAYS TO CHRISTMAS: 21" → three jobs to do now, each with a quick photo cut, light snow overlay, handwritten "you've got this" → CTA. ~15 s.
5. **"Weekly or fortnightly?" this-or-that** — split screen, two routines side by side, quick facts flip, ends "Which are you? Comment below." ~12 s.
6. **Real footage wrap (needs her clips)** — her own 5–10 s phone clips cut to the beat, mask-reveal captions, a satisfying slow-motion moment, cinemagraph end card. The strongest performer if she can film a little.

Before→after (only with her real photos) stays available as a template inside any of these.
